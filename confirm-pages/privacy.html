Perseveo (“the app”) is a habit tracker for iOS, Android and the web, made by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app handles, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • You need an account. We keep your email, display name and your habit data on our backend so it syncs between your devices.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share your habits with anyone.
  • The free iOS and Android apps show Google AdMob banners. Google may use device identifiers for ads, only as your consent choices allow. The web version has no ads.
  • Reminders and home-screen widgets work on your device only.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from the app at any time (Profile → Delete account).

1. Your account

To use the app you sign in with one of:

  • Email and password. We store your email address, your display name (if you give one) and a securely hashed password. We send you an email to confirm your address.
  • Sign in with Google. Google tells us your email address and name, and confirms your identity. We do not receive your Google password or access your other Google data.
  • Sign in with Apple. Apple tells us your email address (or a private relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”) and, the first time, your name. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

We also keep the date you joined and which sign-in method you use. The Profile screen shows this information, and you can change your display name and (for email accounts) your password there.

Your sign-in session is stored on your device in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore-backed storage on Android).

2. Your habits and progress

The app works offline first: everything is saved on your device. When you are signed in and online, it also syncs to your account so your data survives a reinstall or a new phone:

  • Habits: name, emoji, colour, schedule, start date, reminder time, and whether a habit is archived.
  • Check-ins: which habit you completed on which day.
  • Game progress: XP, level, streak stats and achievements.
  • Saved quotes: which daily quotes you marked with ♥.

We use this data only to run the app for you (show, sync and restore your habits and progress). We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with advertisers. The daily quote itself is the same for everyone; fetching it reveals nothing about you.

3. Where your data is stored

Our backend runs on Supabase (database, authentication and server functions), which acts as our data processor. All traffic between the app and the backend is encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can only read and change its own data. Like most online services, Supabase may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and to operate the service.

Your data may be processed outside your country. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Ads (iOS and Android)

Perseveo is free thanks to a few small banner ads from Google AdMob. Ads appear only in the habit list and on a habit's detail screen. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or on iOS the IDFA if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country / city level) may be derived;
  • information about the device and app (model, OS version, app version) and your interactions with ads;
  • diagnostics about ad loading and performance.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions that require it). Before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). If you do not consent to personalised ads, you get limited or non-personalised ads. If the consent tool says no ads may be requested, none are.
  • Tracking on iOS. After the consent step, iOS asks whether Perseveo may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are non-personalised and the IDFA is not shared. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).

Google's use of this data is governed by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ads are never shown with your habits' content, and we never send your habits to Google.

5. Reminders and notifications

Habit reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device from your habits' reminder times. They are not sent through our servers or any push service. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.

6. Home-screen widgets

The widgets show today's due habits and today's quote. The app shares this snapshot with the widget through your device's local storage only (an App Group on iOS, app preferences on Android). Checking off a habit on a widget is applied by the app like any other check-in. Nothing extra leaves your device because of the widgets.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

8. How long we keep data, and deleting your account

We keep your account and synced data for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Danger zone → Delete account (you need to be online). This permanently deletes your check-ins, habits, game progress, saved quotes, profile and login from our backend, and wipes the app's data and scheduled reminders from that device.

If you can't use the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the address of your account and ask us to delete it; we will do so within 30 days. Backups of the backend roll over and are gone within a short period after deletion.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict some processing. Most of this you can do in the app (edit your name, delete your account). For anything else, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (GDPR): running the app and syncing your account is necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract); personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw; security logs rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

10. Children

Perseveo is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
